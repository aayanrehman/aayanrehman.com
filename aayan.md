## What he builds

### Agentic AI growth systems
AI agents that run growth end to end. They research the market, launch the campaigns, follow up with every lead and book the meeting, with a person approving the decisions that matter. Example: agents that confirm, remind and follow up with every event registrant until they book a consult.

### B2B AI implementation
He sits with the team, maps how the work moves, and replaces manual steps with AI that runs inside existing tools (Microsoft Power Platform, Copilot Studio, Claude, or custom code). Then he tests it with users, documents it and hands it off with governance and SOPs. Example: AI agents for Voya Financial's Sales Strategy team that took over proposal intake, pricing and cost comparison.

### AI ads and lead generation
AI studies competitor ads across a market, turns one shoot into a library of creative, and builds campaigns from code, so research that took weeks takes an afternoon. Example: 721 competitor ads researched and scored for $4.36 of compute, producing 26 ads, 2 reels and 28 hooks.

### Custom CRMs and automations
Systems a business owns instead of rents: a CRM shaped around how the team sells and serves, with reminders, reporting and follow-ups automated. Example: a branded client CRM that replaced a paid subscription tool.

## How he runs an implementation

1. Interview the people doing the work (sales, pricing, operations).
2. Map the as-is and to-be process, every handoff, and where time and accuracy are lost.
3. Decide what should be AI and what should stay deterministic: rules and math stay testable; AI handles reading, drafting and judgment calls, with human approval.
4. Build inside the existing stack.
5. Run user acceptance testing, set data governance and access rules, write SOPs, and hand off.
