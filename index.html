Why I do this

When ChatGPT came out, the people around me were asking whether AI would take their jobs. I used it to build a website for my teacher's wife.

That's when I saw it. The knowledge is free now, and the tools get better every month, but most businesses still don't have anyone to put them to work. Whoever does the implementing captures the value.

So that's what I do. I implemented AI agents inside an enterprise at Voya Financial, co-founded Waterfall Growth to build growth systems for service firms, and share what I build on LinkedIn and YouTube so other owners can see what's possible.

Questions people ask

Who is Aayan Rehman?

Aayan Rehman is a young entrepreneur in the AI space. He builds agentic AI growth systems for businesses and is the co-founder of Waterfall Growth. His work covers B2B AI implementation, AI ads and lead generation, and custom CRMs and automations.

What are agentic AI growth systems?

Agentic AI growth systems are AI agents that handle the work of growing a business end to end: researching the market, running campaigns, following up with every lead and booking meetings, with a person approving the decisions that matter.

What is B2B AI implementation?

B2B AI implementation is putting AI to work inside a company's real processes. Aayan maps how work moves through a team, finds the manual steps, and replaces them with AI agents that run in the tools the team already uses, then tests and documents them so they keep working.

What is the difference between AI automation and agentic AI?

AI automation follows fixed steps someone designed in advance. Agentic AI can decide the next step itself: it reads the situation, chooses an action, uses tools and checks its work. Aayan builds agentic systems with guardrails, so a person approves the decisions that matter.
